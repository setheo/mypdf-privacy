MyPDF Notes – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Summary

MyPDF Notes does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

What the app does with your data

  • Your PDFs, notebooks, annotations and comments are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Camera: used only when you choose to scan a paper document. Images are processed on your device and saved as PDF files on your device.
  • Text recognition (OCR): runs on your device. No images or text are sent to any server.
  • The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions or support: open an issue on GitHub.
